For Zonar customers

Refer a fleet, extend your agreement, get up to 2 months free

When you refer a company, you can also extend your own Zonar agreement. Extend 24 months and get 1 month free on each of your telematics and camera subscriptions. Extend 36 months and get 2 months free. The more vehicles you run with Zonar, the more you save.

Your credit doesn't depend on the company you referred. Sign the extension and the credit is yours, even if they don't become a customer. Your gift card is paid once they sign. Automatic 12-month renewals don't count, so extensions start at 24 months.

Your free service is applied as a credit spread across your next 12 invoices, starting right after you sign.

Who qualifies

Quick check before you refer

Counts as a referral

  • A business that's new to Zonar
  • Signs an agreement of 12 months or more within 30 days of being contacted
  • Pays its first invoice and stays active for 90 days
  • You were the first person to refer them

Doesn't count

  • A current or past Zonar customer
  • A company already working with a Zonar fleet specialist, demo, or trial
  • A company that doesn't sign, or cancels within 90 days

Public sector

Government and public sector (SLED) organizations can qualify. Rewards are applied as a credit on the next invoice. We don't send payments to public sector employees, so if you work in the public sector your reward is also issued as a credit. The same goes for anyone whose employer doesn't allow them to accept gift cards.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Who can refer?

Zonar customers, partners, and industry contacts. If you know a business that runs vehicles, you can refer them.

How many companies can I refer?

As many as you like. There's no limit.

When do I get my reward?

Once the company has been an active customer for 90 days, the referral qualifies. Rewards are sent as e-gift cards within 30 days after that.

Does the company I refer get anything?

Yes. They earn a reward too, from $50 to $500 based on their fleet size. It doubles if they sign a 2-year contract and triples on a 3-year contract.

What if someone else referred the same company?

The reward goes to the first person who submitted the referral.

What if they cancel?

If the company cancels within 90 days of activation, the referral doesn't qualify.

Can I refer a government agency or school district?

Yes. Public sector referrals earn an invoice credit in place of a gift card.

Can I earn more as a Zonar customer?

Yes. Extend your own agreement when you refer and you'll get free service on each of your telematics and camera subscriptions: 1 month free for 24 more months, or 2 months free for 36 more months. You still get your gift card once the company you referred signs, and the credit applies whether or not they do.

What if I'm not allowed to accept a gift card?

Check the box on the form and we'll apply your reward as a credit on your company's account, the same way we handle public sector referrals.

How do I check on a referral?

We'll email you as it moves along. Zonar customers can also see status in the app under Refer & earn.

All referrals are subject to Zonar's verification and approval. Extension credits have no cash value, can't be transferred, and any unused credit is forfeited if the agreement ends early. Zonar employees aren't eligible. Read the full program terms.
